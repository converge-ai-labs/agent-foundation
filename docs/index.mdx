<Intro title="Agent Foundation" action={{ text: "Choose your path", href: "/overview/choose-your-path/" }}>

The open-source, self-hosted foundation for AI agents. Deploy the managed Service, embed the Harness library in your application, or work interactively with Harness UI.

</Intro>

<ArchitectureOverview alt="a13n Service and Harness connect SDKs, HTTP API, Console, and Harness UI with environments, memory, models, web data, and MCP connections." />

<Section title="Products">
<Products>
<Product icon="service" title="Service" audience="Teams and applications" href="/a13n-service/" start="/a13n-service/get-started/">

A self-hosted platform with identities, workspaces, durable execution, and saved conversations, used through Console or the HTTP API.

</Product>
<Product icon="harness" title="Harness" audience="Python developers" href="/a13n-harness/" start="/a13n-harness/getting-started/">

A library that composes agents from instructions, tools, and capabilities and runs them in your process. Your application owns storage and access.

</Product>
<Product icon="harness-ui" title="Harness UI" audience="Individuals and small teams" href="/a13n-harness-ui/" start="/a13n-harness-ui/setup/">

A terminal and browser workbench for working with agents in your repositories, with shared conversations, files, and terminals.

</Product>
</Products>
</Section>
